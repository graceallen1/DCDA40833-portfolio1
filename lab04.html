Lab 4: Tableau Dashboard

Social Media Analytics Dashboard

Reflection

These graphs show social media engagement trends across different platforms and regions. The goal was to show which platforms perform best in specific geographic areas and how engagement varies from platform to platform such as likes, views, and interaction. Making a visual for this data helped show patterns that would be difficult to see in just the data set. I chose this because in Strategic Communications, social media is super relevant and in todays world is always relevent. When I saw this data set as an option I thought it would be really interesting to see.
