Lab 4: Tableau Dashboard

Social Media Analytics Dashboard

Reflection

These graphs show social media engagement trends across different platforms and regions. The goal of this visualization was to highlight which social media platforms perform best in different geographic areas and how engagement varies depending on the platform. The dataset compared things like likes, views, and interactions, which helped show how audiences engage with content across multiple platforms. By turning the dataset into visual graphs and a dashboard, it became much easier to identify patterns and trends that would be difficult to notice when looking at raw numbers alone. Visualizing the data allowed me to quickly compare engagement levels and understand which platforms might be more effective in different parts of the world.

I chose this dataset because social media is extremely relevant to my major in Strategic Communications. In today’s world, social media platforms are one of the main ways that brands communicate with their audiences, build relationships, and measure success. Understanding how engagement varies across platforms is something that is constantly discussed in marketing, advertising, and public relations. When I saw this dataset as an option, I thought it would be interesting to explore because it connects directly to real-world communication strategies. It also made the assignment feel more meaningful since social media trends are something that affect both businesses and everyday users.

At the same time, if I am being honest, I struggled quite a bit with this assignment. I ended up turning it in later than the due date because I needed help from some of my peers to understand how to use Tableau effectively. When we first went over Tableau in class, I was not able to download the full application and had to use the web version instead. Because of that, it was harder for me to follow along with the steps during class, and I felt like I started the assignment already behind. The interface felt overwhelming at first, and I had trouble figuring out how to organize the data and build the graphs correctly.

Once I started working with classmates and experimenting with the features more, I slowly began to understand how Tableau works. Seeing how other people approached the assignment helped me learn how to structure my visualizations and connect the different pieces of the dashboard. Even though the process was frustrating at times, it helped me realize how powerful data visualization tools can be when they are used correctly. Tableau makes it possible to turn complicated datasets into visuals that communicate information much more clearly.

Overall, even though I had a difficult time with the assignment, I do think my final graphs and dashboard turned out well. I am still open to feedback because there were definitely moments where I felt unsure about my choices while building the visuals. However, working through the challenges helped me gain a better understanding of Tableau and data visualization in general. This assignment showed me how important it is to present data in a way that is clear, engaging, and easy to interpret, especially in fields like Strategic Communications where data often needs to support decisions and storytelling.
